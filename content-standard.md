# NWH Reel Content Standard

Use this prompt when preparing the campaign in ChatGPT. Create one Reel at a time so each post has a distinct angle.

## Campaign purpose

Prepare 90 distinct Facebook Reels for a 30-day test for Narmae Wellness Hub® (NWH). The audience is Philippine spa owners, managers, therapists, and wellness clients.

## Daily editorial roles (PHT)

- **9:00 AM — Awakening:** Start a meaningful conversation, surface a familiar situation, or help the audience reconsider an assumption. Do not turn every morning post into a sales pitch.
- **1:00 PM — Education:** Teach practical knowledge viewers can apply in their work or business. A short Reel can demonstrate expertise but must not be presented as a replacement for professional training or consulting.
- **6:00 PM — Promotion:** Explain the actual NWH offer, who it is for, what it includes, how the engagement works, its boundaries, and the next step.

## Practical execution standard

Every educational Reel must answer: **What is going wrong? Why does it happen? How exactly can it be corrected?** Use this sequence:

1. Show a real, believable situation inside a spa business.
2. Identify exactly where the process breaks down and who or what is affected.
3. Explain the operational, financial, workforce, or client-service consequence.
4. Demonstrate the correction step by step. Specify who is responsible, what to do, when and how to do it, what checklist or record to use, and how the owner verifies completion.
5. Give a realistic example. Identify illustrative numbers as examples.
6. Connect the lesson to a relevant NWH service while separating the owner's or staff's internal work from NWH's external support.

Management guidance must demonstrate a fair, documented, and legally appropriate process. Avoid generic advice without implementation steps.

## NWH services

- **Staffing Support:** Help identify workforce requirements and address sourcing gaps.
- **Therapist Training:** Provide structured practical instruction and competency development.
- **Spa Operations Consulting:** Review workflows, develop procedures, clarify responsibilities, and establish suitable monitoring systems.

Do not invent service terms, prices, guarantees, deliverables, or policies. If an offer detail is not confirmed, mark it **[CONFIRM]**. Free content demonstrates expertise; paid services provide structured support, training, implementation, or professional guidance.

## Visual and language rules

- Natural Taglish and conversational pacing; culturally relatable Philippine situations.
- Full-frame vertical, natural live-action-style footage in recognizable Philippine spa or wellness settings.
- Keep people faceless on camera: backs, hands, silhouettes, and over-the-shoulder views.
- Use realistic lighting, movement, sound, gestures, and everyday interactions.
- Exclude foreign-looking footage, AI-generated people or images, animation, robotic narration, stiff acting, decorative borders, and obvious template frames.
- Do not use U.S. industry statistics as evidence about the Philippine market.
- Make each Reel meaningfully different; do not recycle scripts with changed wording.

## Required deliverable for each Reel

Return: post role and day; theme; distinct angle; strong opening hook; visual direction; natural Taglish script or voice-over; caption; CTA; role-specific teaching or offer details; and a Pixabay video search phrase matched to the theme and visual direction. Use the same supplied “Bossa Nova Soulful Music” track by andriih (Pixabay item 630197) as quiet background music across all Reels; do not suggest a replacement song. For education, include the full practical sequence. For promotion, state the real offer, included service, process, boundaries, and next step clearly without hiding it behind a vague cliffhanger.

## Production and publication boundary

1. Prepare content for all 90 Reels in batches.
2. Source suitable stock footage and licensed background music; retain license evidence.
3. Edit vertical MP4s with voice-over, subtitles, and music.
4. Upload finished Reels to the Narmae Wellness Hub® Facebook Page and schedule them in Meta Business Suite.

Meta Business Suite publishes videos that have already been uploaded and scheduled. It does not create scripts, download media, edit videos, or upload new Reels. This initial 30-day workflow uses batch production and Meta Business Suite scheduling; it does not require Make.com.

## Pixabay media matching

For every Reel, search Pixabay using that post's theme, angle, and visual direction. Choose a genuine live-action clip that fits the Filipino setting and faceless visual rules. Use the shared campaign soundtrack above for all posts; keep it beneath Taglish dialogue. Review each video candidate instead of treating search results as approved assets. Record the video source and the soundtrack's download evidence. Check the item's content type and any third-party rights, such as recognizable people, logos, or property.
